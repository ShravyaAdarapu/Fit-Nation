# Fit Nation — information to collect from the gym

Please fill in one branch sheet for **each of the six locations**. If an item does not apply, mark it “not available” rather than leaving it ambiguous. Website entries are only published as confirmed after the gym approves them.

## 1. Brand and ownership — once for the whole gym
- Official display name and legal billing name.
- Original logo: SVG or transparent PNG, plus light/dark versions if available. The website currently uses the actual crest downloaded from the official Instagram profile; send the original for sharp display.
- Brand colours, tagline, brief history, owner-approved description and official social accounts.
- Main customer enquiry email, phone and WhatsApp; domain name if already owned.
- Names and authorised login emails for website owner, branch managers and enquiry handlers. Never send passwords.

## 2. Branch identity and exact location — repeat six times
- Branch name, neighbourhood, complete street address, floor, building name, landmark and postcode.
- Exact Google Maps share link for that branch. Confirm the map pin points to the real entrance, not just the area.
- Latitude/longitude if available, entrance photo and exterior/signboard photo.
- Parking location, bike/car parking availability and charges, lift and step-free access.
- Nearby metro/bus stop, walking directions and any hard-to-find entrance instructions.
- Direct branch phone, WhatsApp and enquiry email; person/team who handles leads.

## 3. Timings and visits
- Opening and closing time for every day, including Sundays, split morning/evening sessions and break times.
- Last entry time, holiday hours, weekly closing days and planned closures.
- Reception hours versus gym access hours if different.
- General trainer floor coverage, personal trainer shifts, women-only sessions if offered.
- Typical quieter and busiest periods, with the source/date of that estimate.
- Visit/trial slots, booking lead time, cancellation rules and who confirms a request.

## 4. Equipment inventory — exact for each branch
- Free weights: dumbbell weight range, quantities, barbells, plates, benches, squat/power racks and lifting platforms.
- Strength machines: machine name, manufacturer, model if known and count. Include Smith machine, cable stations and assisted machines only if present.
- Cardio: treadmills, bikes, ellipticals, rowers, stair machines and counts.
- Functional equipment: kettlebells, resistance bands, medicine balls, battle ropes, sled/turf, boxing bags, pull-up rigs and mats, where present.
- Equipment photographs, designated training zones and any usage restrictions.
- Temporary out-of-service equipment and the staff member responsible for keeping the list current.

## 5. Trainers and classes
- Current roster assigned to this branch; approved name, photograph and professional bio for each trainer.
- Role, qualifications/certifications with issuing body, years of experience, specialties and languages.
- Shift or bookable hours, personal-training availability, whether included or paid separately, and female trainer availability.
- Group classes actually offered: title, coach, days, time, duration, capacity, beginner suitability and booking requirements.
- Personal training/package pricing and verified nutrition professional details if nutrition services are offered.
- Professional contact/booking route; do not publish trainers’ private contact details without their agreement.

## 6. Facilities and membership rules
- AC/ventilation, drinking water, washrooms, showers, changing rooms, lockers, parking, Wi-Fi, accessibility and any additional facilities.
- Opening access requirements, age policy, guest policy, footwear/dress requirements and what a first-time visitor should bring.
- Whether beginners receive an induction; women-friendly provisions, dedicated areas/timings and any restrictions.
- Which services are included versus charged separately.

## 7. Plans, prices and payments
- Monthly, quarterly, half-yearly and annual prices for every available plan at each branch.
- Joining/registration fee, taxes, deposits, valid offers and expiry dates; total payable amount.
- Single-branch versus multi-branch access: exactly which locations each plan permits.
- Trial/day-pass policy, cost and eligibility; personal-training add-ons and class inclusions.
- Renewal, freeze, transfer, cancellation and refund rules, with owner-approved wording.
- Existing billing/membership software, supported payment provider, invoice requirements and renewal process.
- Payment-provider account setup must use its secure onboarding flow; do not send card details or secret keys in this sheet.

## 8. Real photography, reviews and public content
- Original high-resolution exterior/interior images for every branch, plus equipment, staff and class photos.
- Short gym-tour video and any approved footage. Identify which branch appears in each file.
- Approved trainer/member photo usage; owner-approved testimonials with attribution and source.
- Google Business Profile link for each branch; genuine review source and date. No invented ratings or outcomes.
- Existing FAQs, current promotions, opening announcements and local events.

## 9. Operations and website administration
- How members, enquiries, bookings, attendance, payments and renewals are handled today.
- Who receives each branch’s enquiries, expected response time, escalation contact and preferred notification channel.
- Whether bookings are requests or immediately confirmed; real capacity/slot rules before instant booking is enabled.
- Which branch managers may view/edit which records, and owner-only actions.
- Who will keep hours, equipment, staff, class schedules and offers current.
- Privacy contact, data retention/deletion process and owner-approved privacy/terms/refund documents.
